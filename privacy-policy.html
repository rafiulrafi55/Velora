How Velora handles your information

Velora only collects the data needed to manage events, guests, and check-ins. Recent app changes also adjusted account-deletion and data-cleanup behavior, so this page reflects the current handling of user data.

Effective Date July 2026
Applies To Velora web app

1. Privacy Policy

1.1 Information We Collect

We collect guest names, contact details, RSVP status, dietary preferences, seating preferences, and account information required to authenticate users and manage their events.

1.2 How We Use Information

Your data is used to create, manage, and organize events; track guest lists and check-ins; support account access; and keep the app secure.

1.3 Data Protection

We use access controls and app-level safeguards to reduce unauthorized access to personal information. Sensitive actions such as account deletion require confirmation before they proceed.

1.4 Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We only share data with third parties when required to provide the service, support a requested feature, or comply with legal obligations.

2. Terms of Service

2.1 User Responsibilities

Users must provide accurate information and use Velora only for legitimate event-planning purposes.

2.2 Acceptable Use

You must not attempt unauthorized access, alter data you do not own, or misuse guest information.

2.3 Service Availability

We aim to keep the service available, but uninterrupted uptime is not guaranteed.

2.4 Limitation of Liability

Velora is not liable for indirect losses caused by service interruptions, data-entry mistakes, or misuse of the system.

3. Recent Privacy-Related Changes

  • Account deletion now removes the signed-in user’s profile and related records from the app.
  • Guest deletion and cleanup flows are limited to data owned by the current user.
  • The changes above affect how Velora handles personal data, but they do not introduce new third-party sharing or tracking.

4. Contact Us

For privacy questions, corrections, or deletion requests, email us at rafiulrafi55@gmail.com.

If we make future changes that materially affect data collection, deletion, or sharing, this page will be updated to match the new behavior.
